# Privacy summary

This text is a product draft and must be reviewed for the jurisdictions where the service launches.

- Workout history is private by default.
- New workouts are backed up automatically and privately to the cloud from
  the first save on — only the athlete can see this data, and it is
  entirely separate from joining the community (posting, a public profile,
  the feed). This automatic backup can be turned off at any time in
  Settings, and turning it off never affects anything already saved on the
  device itself.
- A community post is created only after the athlete selects a result and an audience.
- Shared snapshots contain the workout name, result, date, Rx/Scaled state where relevant, and comparison score.
- Posts can optionally include photos, which are stored privately and shown
  only to whoever the post's audience allows.
- Comments on posts are visible to whoever can see the post itself.
- Following another member is visible to that member (and, depending on
  their own privacy settings, to people who can see their profile) — it is
  not anonymous.
- Members control a set of visibility toggles on their own profile: whether
  the profile is visible to the club at all, and whether workout results,
  personal records, achievements, attendance, upcoming bookings, and
  leaderboard placement are shown to others. Separate toggles control
  whether other members can follow, @mention, or message the athlete.
- Sign-in never collects an email address or any contact detail — a
  community account is created and identified only by an anonymous
  session and the profile handle the athlete chooses.
- Bodyweight, body measurements, session notes, scaling notes, partner tags, and backup files are not published.
- Users can block people, report posts, export local data, and request account deletion.
- A deletion request hides the profile and posts immediately and schedules permanent account removal after 30 days.
- Cloud infrastructure processes account and workout data to provide authentication, synchronization, and community features.
- Club admins can see a directory of every member's handle, display name,
  role, and join date, for club administration purposes.

Before launch, add the operator's legal identity, contact details, hosting region, retention periods, subprocessors, lawful basis, age requirement, and applicable data-subject rights.
