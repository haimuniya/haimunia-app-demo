# Community rules and terms draft

This draft requires legal review before public launch.

- Users must be old enough to consent under the laws that apply to them.
- Workout results are user-provided and are not medical advice or certified competition results.
- Harassment, impersonation, spam, privacy violations, and dangerous or illegal content are prohibited.
- Users retain ownership of their content and grant the service permission to display content they explicitly publish.
- Reported content may be restricted while it is reviewed.
- Accounts may be restricted for repeated or serious violations.
- Users can remove individual shares or request account deletion.
